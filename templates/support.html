Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take assessments as a guest without creating an account?
Yes. DocHub features a public-first architecture. You can start any cognitive screening immediately. Your progress and recent attempt are cached in your browser and can be permanently linked by signing up or logging in.
Are these assessments medical or clinical diagnoses?
No. DocHub provides AI-assisted screening and cognitive learning insights. Results highlight potential areas for targeted classroom accommodations and are not a substitute for formal neuropsychological or medical diagnoses.
How does DocHub analyze handwriting or stylus drawing?
Our Dysgraphia neural network tracks digital stylus and touch vector coordinates in real time, calculating mean turning angle variance, line straightness deviation, and velocity stability.
