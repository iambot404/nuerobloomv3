SIGNAL INTEGRATION

How DocHub processes an assessment

Invisible intelligence operating in real time: capturing interaction signals, passing them through specialized neural networks, and generating evidence-based educator insights.

01

Interactive Signal Capture

Browser captures touch paths, vector coordinates, millisecond response times, and phoneme choices.

02

Feature Extraction

Computes 20+ specialized cognitive features: turning angle variance, line deviation, speed fatigue, error distribution.

03

Neural Forward Pass

Trained deep learning architectures synthesize features into calibrated impairment risk probabilities.

04

Multi-Signal Classification

Categorizes risk thresholds into None, Low, Medium, or High with confidence percentiles.

05

Actionable Guidance

Selects personalized accommodations, multisensory learning strategies, and classroom recommendations.
